Support

If location, background anchor watch, or alarms are not working as expected, start with these checks.

Safety notice: DingHai is an anchor-watch aid and cannot replace a human watch, compliant marine equipment, or independent alarms.

Unable to place an anchor

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → DingHai.
  2. Set location access to Always and enable Precise Location.
  3. Return to an open area and wait for DingHai to show a reliable position before placing the anchor.

No background alert

  1. Confirm that anchor watch is still active and has not been ended.
  2. In Settings → Notifications → DingHai, allow notifications and sounds.
  3. Check system volume, Focus modes, and connected Bluetooth audio devices.
  4. Do not force-quit DingHai from the app switcher while anchor watch is active.

Location remains unavailable

GPS can be delayed inside a cabin, around dense metal structures, or where the sky view is limited. Move to a more open location and confirm that Location Services are enabled. Do not rely on the app as your only anchor-watch method before a reliable position is available.

Contact the development team

If the issue continues, open a GitHub Issue with your device model, iOS version, reproduction steps, and the status shown in the app.

Do not post real vessel positions, movement tracks, Apple Account details, or other sensitive information publicly.
